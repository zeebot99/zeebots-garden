# 🎯 Choose your goal
Where do you want to be?
- **A)** I want someone else to manage any updates & code (maintenance) my website may need and just post my writing --> [[4 - Indexes/4.1 - Paths/How to make a website#A) No maintenance platforms for writers\|#A) No maintenance platforms for writers]] 
- **B)** I want someone else to manage any maintenance my site may need and just post my art[^1] --> [[4 - Indexes/4.1 - Paths/How to make a website#B) No maintenance platforms for artists\|#B) No maintenance platforms for artists]] 
- **C)** I can do *light* maintenance (customization options with some code), but I mainly want to work on what's on the site --> [[4 - Indexes/4.1 - Paths/How to make a website#C) Light maintenance -> smooth sailing\|#C) Light maintenance -> smooth sailing]] 
- **D)** I can do heavy maintenance (code everything myself) for my site *to start out*, but after that, I want to focus on what's on it [[4 - Indexes/4.1 - Paths/How to make a website#D) Heavy maintenance -> smooth sailing\|#D) Heavy maintenance -> smooth sailing]] 
- **E)** I want to own and manage my website and everything on it, I'll take care of any maintenance needed --> [[4 - Indexes/4.1 - Paths/How to make a website#E) Own everything\|#E) Own everything]] 
- Give me a list of options and I'll pick one --> [[4 - Indexes/4.1 - Paths/How to make a website#All the ways to make a site\|#All the ways to make a site]] 
[Need help picking?](https://newsletter.ownyourweb.site/archive/issue-04/)

# A) No maintenance platforms for writers
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.com/) / [Squarespace](https://squarespace.com/) / [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/) / [Weebly](https://www.weebly.com/)
	- If you want to blog to make money, definitely go with one of these, as that'll get you the most ✨professional✨ results.
	- Wix is an Israeli company, please [participate in the boycott](https://boycottwix.org/) by using something else!
- Blogger
- Platforms where you write then post (themes optional):
	- [Ghost](https://ghost.org), [Pagecord](https://pagecord.com), [Write.as](https://write.as/), [Substack](https://substack.com), [Mataroa](https://mataroa.blog/), 
	- the weblog feature on [omg.lol](https://home.omg.lol/), and
	- [Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com) or [Dreamwidth](https://www.dreamwidth.org/) 
	- [Bear Blog](https://bearblog.dev/) or [Pika](https://pika.page/) 
- Site builders like [Framer](https://www.framer.com/), [Statamic](https://statamic.com/), or [Publii](https://getpublii.com/) 
- [Medium](https://medium.com) 
- [Forestry.md](https://forestry.md/) 
# B) No maintenance platforms for artists
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.com/) / [Squarespace](https://squarespace.com/) / [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/) / [Weebly](https://www.weebly.com/)
	- If you want to sell your art on your website, definitely go with Shopify or Weebly for their shop-related features.
	- Wix is an Israeli company, please [participate in the boycott](https://boycottwix.org/) by using something else!
- [Cow Sites](https://cowsites.net/) 
- Site builders: [Framer](https://www.framer.com/), [Statamic](https://statamic.com/), or [Publii](https://getpublii.com/) 
- [Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com) or [Dreamwidth](https://www.dreamwidth.org/) 
- Drag-and-drop editing: [Lurk](https://lurk.me/) or [mmm.page](https://mmm.page/) 
# C) Light maintenance -> smooth sailing
- Templates! Just change some code values and you're good to go:
	- [StrawberryStarter](https://strawberrystarter.neocities.org/) is an [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) one where the site is the documentation
	- I like these Astro ones: [Blahg](https://blahg.netlify.app/) and [Sphere](https://astro-sphere-demo.vercel.app/) 
- Simple static site generators: [Faircamp](https://faircamp.org/) and [Makko](https://makko.starlightnet.work/) 
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) with the [Digital Garden plugin](https://docs.forestry.md/) 
# D) Heavy maintenance -> smooth sailing
- [Chyrp Lite](https://chyrplite.net/) 
- Static site generators: [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/), [Gatsby](https://www.gatsbyjs.com/), [Astro](https://astro.build/) 
- [Neocities](https://neocities.org) and its derivatives ([Nekoweb](https://nekoweb.org/) and [Reocities](https://reocities.xyz/))
# E) Own everything
- Self-hosting options:
	- Getting started with self-hosting:
		- https://knhash.in/gentle-guide-to-self-hosting/
		- https://rai.so/vps101-renting-a-slice-of-a-computer-on-the-internet/
		- https://blog.basementcommunity.com/everything-i-do-when-hosting-a-website/
		- https://blog.tk-web.quest/posts/2026-04-16-selfhosting-guide/
- [Midnight.pub](https://midnight.pub/)
