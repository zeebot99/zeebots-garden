# doing your own curation can help with discovery
List your favorite or most visited pages on your website so people can have an idea of what's on there. It encourages exploration and gives the visitor an entry point.

This works better than [[6 - Main Notes/pagination\|pagination]], because [[6 - Main Notes/The structure of a traditional blog is not user-friendly\|The structure of a traditional blog is not user-friendly]].
