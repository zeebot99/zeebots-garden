# Your notes are where you think, not something to think about
>[!quote]+
>As an extension of our own memory, the slip-box is the medium we think in, not something we think about

Use your [[6 - Main Notes/knowledge management\|knowledge management]] system as a thinking tool. Don't spend all your time working on it; work inside it.
