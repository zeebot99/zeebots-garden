>[!quote]+ Literature Notes are the notes you make as comments to the text you have selected or marked down.
>lit note: annotations

>[!quote]+ Tip: It’s best to write down more information than later necessary.

>[!quote]+ Permanent notes are stand-alone ideas that can be made without any direct context to other sourced information
