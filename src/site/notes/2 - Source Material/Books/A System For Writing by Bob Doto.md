# ✍🏾 summary
To work with your thoughts, you should:
1. Think.
2. Write it down.
3. Transform that thought into something else.

[[6 - Main Notes/Writing clarifies your thinking\|Writing clarifies your thinking]] which leads to deeper understanding.

[[6 - Main Notes/Names have power\|Names have power]]. Name your note something that describes its contents.
>[!example] Apples and edibility << All apples are edible

basic main note: one idea and link to another idea

The real power in the [[6 - Main Notes/Zettelkasten\|Zettelkasten]] system is the productivity, the increased output that comes from incubating your ideas.
