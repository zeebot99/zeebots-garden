# summary
Act on good impulses when you feel them without overthinking. Don't tell yourself you need to do something else first, or chastise yourself for thoughts that go against that instinct, just act when you feel it. If you include the time you spend thinking about a task and the time you spend stressing about it in the time it takes to get it done, you'll see just how much time is wasted. Just do the thing!
